About Find SuperBus

Find your bus. Find your route. Travel smarter.

Project
purpose.

Find SuperBus is a B.Tech project prototype designed to demonstrate how passengers could discover bus routes, departures, seat availability, and journey options through a single interface. It is an offline-first public transportation demonstration.

Bus routes, schedules, fares, service statuses, and seat information shown in this project are simulated demonstration data. This project is not connected to government transport schedules, GPS, or live transit services.

FEATURES

  • Bus search and route discovery
  • Journey planning and sample departures
  • Interactive seat availability
  • Favorites and recent searches
  • Notifications and accessibility settings
  • Responsive offline demo interface
